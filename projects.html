PeacePlayers CRM Strategy

Audited a nonprofit's HubSpot CRM, diagnosed weak engagement as a targeting problem, and designed the segmentation, scoring, and lifecycle journeys to act on it.

Result: Diagnostic
Tools: HubSpot, SQL

CAMPAIGN ANALYTICS

Marketing Campaign Response & Segmentation

Analyzed 2,240 customers to find who actually responds to a marketing campaign, and tested whether web activity predicts customer value. Found it doesn't: the highest spenders browsed the least.

Result: Targeting Strategy
Tools: R, SQL, Tableau

CONTENT STRATEGY

Predicting TikTok Virality

A model on 12,345 videos predicting organic shares from pre-publication features. I owned the business implications: what creators, brands, and platforms should do with it.

Result: Predictive Modeling
Tools: LASSO, Random Forest

CAUSAL INFERENCE

Cross-App Spillover on Kakao

A causal study of how adopting a hit game shifts usage of other apps on Kakao. Built the PSM/DID pipeline and found the spillover is asymmetric: it flows to games, not to social.

Result: Platform Strategy
Tools: R, PSM/DID
